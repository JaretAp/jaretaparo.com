My Journey So Far

About eight years ago, I built my first PC. What started as overclocking CPUs and GPUs for a few extra frames quickly turned into a deeper fascination with the hardware itself. I discovered I enjoyed tinkering, upgrading, and rebuilding more than I enjoyed playing the games.

That curiosity expanded into building websites with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript. Learning JavaScript showed me the power of programming, which led me to explore Python, C, C++, and Java — diving into loops, functions, classes, and recursion.

To program effectively, I needed to understand how computers communicate, which pulled me into networking: subnets, firewalls, VPNs, and VLANs. From there, cybersecurity was a natural next step — protecting networks against evolving threats.

Today, I’ve come full circle, working hands-on with hardware and software in IT support. I’ve earned CompTIA A+, ITF+, Google IT Support, and ISC² Certified in Cybersecurity credentials, and I’m preparing for my ServiceNow CSA. I’m eager to bring my technical skills, problem-solving mindset, and drive for continuous learning to new challenges in IT support, web development, and cybersecurity.
